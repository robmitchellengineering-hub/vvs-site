## H — Here's Who I Am

### My website
https://vvs.morpheus.nz

### What inspired me, and my why
There is an outcry from concerned citizens worldwide around AI accountability and keeping it
under human control, and there is frustration with agentic coding and workflows. VVS exists
to mitigate all of it.

The site puts the failure mode plainly: in 2026 a rogue AI agent breached Australian
government systems, and the response was an apology and "rogue AIs" — no human named, no one
responsible. VVS closes that gap: every action an AI takes is logged with *who approved it*,
*where it came from*, and *what it meant* — a ledger with a human's name on every line. Not
"the AI did it" — "this person said yes, and here is the record."

### Who I help
VVS is working to become a **global standard of verification** — the way SOC 2 is the
recognised standard for security/compliance attestation, VVS aims to be the standard for
"how verified is this?"

### What the business actually does
1. **The VVS SDK** — software enforcement of the verification standard. A repo adopts it with
   one `valiant.json`; the SDK runs that project's own checks *by name*, breaks what each guard
   protects and requires it to go red (mutation testing), and validates an incident ledger
   where every entry names a RULE and a GUARD. The result: "done" means verified, on every run.
2. **Complex data system analysis & solutions** — consultancy contracts on real-world models
   and data that drive decisions and allocate funding (the climate work is the flagship
   example).
3. **Morpheus.nz** — the "chat to anything" coder: AI-driven full-stack generation and
   multi-target compilation (Windows EXEs, Android APKs, Raspberry Pi OS images, Arduino
   firmware), with verification as a structural gate, not an option.

### The proof engine — verification in grades
VVS climbs a ladder of certainty, and only the top rung is called a guarantee:

1. **numerical** — evidence, not proof (a computation that could be wrong).
2. **symbolic exact** — SymPy, zero tolerance; exact, but only at the cases evaluated.
3. **machine-checked** — Lean 4 + Mathlib; the statement is verified by the theorem prover's
   kernel for *every* case. No `sorry`, no `axiom`, no `native_decide`.

Already proven kernel-checked: **Mitchell's Uniqueness Theorem** (the tadpole FᵀJH is the only
symplectic invariant, for n=1 and general n over any field), the completeness/transitivity
half, and the FFT invariant slices at degrees 1–4.

### The SDK's proof is real adoptions
The SDK ships a **corpus** — 48 invariants, 58 hazards, 24 method rules — signed and dated,
growing after the sale. It is proven by driving real repos (harness-mesh, pacland, morpheus),
never against toy fixtures. Commands: `valiant verify`, `valiant mutate`, `valiant ledger`,
`valiant gate`, `valiant licence`, `valiant ui`, `valiant control --prove`.

### Certification — VDS Certified
Audited, version-scoped, revocable. The standard is open; certification is the audited
product: a signed conformance report, a public registry entry, and a mark that carries its own
scope — so anyone can check it rather than trust it.

1. **Apply** — send the model: a candidate manifest, the evidence bundle, read access.
2. **Run** — the conformance suite: six checks against the public spec → a hash-stamped report.
3. **Publish** — the registry: version, level, deficit, guards proven, report hash, in public.

(The first registry entry is `pacland 0.9 · L3 · m = 4`, labelled as self-certified reference.)

## The scientific research arm — pacland

The science that makes the standard credible, run end-to-end and published:

- **Physics (string landscape / flux vacua):** a 14-file corpus of 5,885 rows under one schema;
  a verified **deficit** (`m = 9`, audited per observable); the theorem tower 6/6 PASS.
- **Quantum:** SEXTANT classifies every quantum claim as **FORCED** (a theorem), **FREE** (a
  label) or **IMPOSSIBLE** (a no-go), and hands back machine-checked proof, not a
  floating-point number. It rides the simulators everyone uses (Stim, Quimb, QuEST) but
  certifies *why* they're correct. 107 Lean files, zero `sorry`/`axiom`.
- **Climate (the worked example):** the site's illustrative framing — 3.78 K ensemble mean →
  3.08 K gated / 2.71 K reference (≈ 0.7–1.1 K of hot-model bias removed); 53 CMIP6 models →
  15 trusted, each exclusion named. (Exact figures set per engagement.)
