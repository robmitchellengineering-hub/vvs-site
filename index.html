AI software

“Done” is a check, not an assertion — and the checks are shown failing.

The Valiant Verification SDK runs a project’s own checks by name, then breaks the thing each guard protects and requires the guard to go red: a guard that cannot be shown to fail proves nothing, and a gate that is skipped, neutral or exits zero with no output is a fail, not a pass — 7 distinct ways a gate can fail, each reported with the reason. It ships the corpus rather than describing it: 41 universal invariants and the 53 hazards that bought them (49 universal; the other 4 deliberately excluded as product-specific), plus 24 operating-method rules covering memory between sessions, verification, build order and reporting — the part every team rewrites badly and nobody publishes. On the adoptions it runs now: 133 guard scripts and 506 mutation proofs on the Morpheus codebase, and 15/15 named gates, 13 recorded hazards each naming its rule and the guard that now catches it, 33/33 mutations caught on our own multi-machine build rig. Every invariant must have a real incident behind it — the publisher refuses a corpus where one does not — and the corpus arrives as a signed, dated feed, so it grows after the sale instead of stopping at the version you installed. That is what a Certified mark is issued from — and it is refused, with the reason, when a single proof is missing.

It also enforces a team's own SOPs. A procedure becomes a named gate; a violation becomes a ledger entry that must name the guard which catches it — so a rule already broken once cannot be quietly broken again, and a “we always do X” that nobody checks shows up as a gap rather than a habit.

The physics work

What the theorem tower changes

The flux sector's only exact structure is the constraint (the D3 tadpole) and the topology. We proved it symbolically and machine-checked it in Lean. The consequence is a no-go: the fifty-year search for an exact-formula unification is mathematically empty.

What is now allowed

  • The debate shifts from vague philosophy to two named, falsifiable premises — the duality group and moduli-independence.
  • Research funding stops chasing provably-dead directions and moves to the constraint and the statistical measure.
  • “Unification” becomes a testable contract, not a hope.
Groundbreaking headlines

The five theorems

  • Mitchell's Uniqueness — the tadpole is the unique bilinear symplectic invariant.
  • Constraint-First — the tadpole generates the entire invariant ring (Weyl FFT).
  • Completeness — the tadpole is a complete invariant, up to content.
  • Full-duality — holds for Sp(b₃) × SL(2,ℤ), not just Sp.
  • Complete Exact Structure — the ToE's exact content = constraints + labels.
The proof of the system
  • Found: 12 of 19 CMIP6 models fail conservation — the worst (CNRM-ESM2-1) leaks at +1.48 W/m², about 2× the Earth's energy imbalance.
  • Localized: salt (ocean) conserves while energy and mass leak across the air–sea boundary — the defect is in the atmosphere–ocean coupling, not the ocean model.
  • Fixed: drift-correction (the field's own fix) restores every model — 19 of 19 conserve exactly after correction.
  • The difference: naive 3.78 K (hot-biased) → strict gate 2.71 K (over-collapses) → repaired 3.08 K, inside the observed 2.6–3.9 K.
