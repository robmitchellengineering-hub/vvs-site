control run - the worst by 1.485 W/m2, 1.93x the Earth's energy imbalance. Salt, which never leaves
the ocean, leaks in only 2 of 19, so the defect is in the air-sea coupling, not the ocean model.

brackets the verified number at [2.71, 3.08] K against AR6's 2.5-4.0 K.

gate can fail. I am asking for one thing: an independent reviewer - my own conformance report passes
